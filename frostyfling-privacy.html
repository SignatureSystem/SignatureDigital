Privacy Policy

Frosty Fling · Effective 14 November 2026

The short version
  • You don't need an account to play, and we never ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Your game progress is saved only on your own device.
  • The game has no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details.

Who we are

Frosty Fling (“the game”) is made by Signature Digital (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have. It applies to the Android version of Frosty Fling available on Google Play.

Information saved on your device

To remember your progress, the game saves the following on your phone or tablet:

  • Your coins, unlocked levels and worlds, and level stars
  • Power-ups and skins you own
  • A record of items you've bought, so they stay unlocked
  • Game statistics and achievements
  • Which tutorials you've already seen, and whether sound is on or off

This information stays on your device. It is not sent to us, and we can't see it. If you uninstall the game or clear its data, it is deleted.

If you have Android backup turned on, your phone may include the game's saved data in your personal device backup to your Google account. Google handles that backup, and you can turn it off in your phone's settings.

In-app purchases

You can buy coins, bundles, skins and other items with real money. All purchases are processed by Google Play.

  • We never receive or store your card number or other payment details.
  • Google gives us a basic record of each order, such as an order number, the item bought, the price, the date and the country. We use it to deliver your items, restore them on a new device, handle refunds and support requests, and keep our accounts.
  • When you open the shop, the game asks Google Play for local prices and checks which items you already own.

Google's use of your information is covered by the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play Terms of Service.

Game updates

When you open the game, it asks Google Play whether a newer version is available so it can offer to update. Google Play handles this check. It doesn't send us any personal information.

What we don't do

  • We don't show ads or use advertising IDs.
  • We don't use analytics, crash reporting or tracking tools.
  • We don't access your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • We don't sell, rent or share personal information with anyone.

Children

Frosty Fling is a casual game for players of all ages. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. All purchases go through Google Play, where parents can require approval with a password or Family Link. If you think a child has sent us personal information, for example in a support email, contact us and we will delete it.

Support emails

If you email us, we'll receive your email address and whatever you write. We only use it to answer you, and we delete it when we no longer need it.

Keeping your information safe

We collect almost no information, and that's the best protection. Purchases use Google Play's secure payment system. Your saved progress is protected by your device's own security.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • Delete your game data: uninstall the game, or go to Settings → Apps → Frosty Fling → Storage → Clear data.
  • Purchases and refunds: manage them in your Google Play account. Google keeps its own record of your orders.
  • Questions or requests: email us. We'll help you see or delete any information we hold about you, depending on the privacy laws where you live.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes, for example by adding new features, we'll update this policy and the date at the top of this page. Big changes will also be mentioned in the game's update notes.
