Privacy Policy

Private Profile Browser
Last updated: 10 November 2026

Private Profile Browser is made by Signature Digital. It lets you browse websites with separate profiles on this device.

What is stored

Each profile stores its own cookies, cache, website storage, and page history on this device. History is kept in the app's private storage. Website storage and cookies stay with that profile until you clear its data or delete it. Android may remove temporary cache files.

The app does not send your browsing history to a Signature Digital server. Websites you visit receive the requests, cookies, and information needed to show their pages. Third-party cookies are enabled for compatibility, so a website and its partners may recognize activity across their services. If you choose a file to upload, that website receives the file.

Website storage by domain is an estimate. Android WebView does not report the complete size of each site's cache or list every cookie domain.

Safe Browsing and search

Android WebView may check the addresses of pages you open with Google Safe Browsing to warn you about dangerous sites. When you type words instead of a web address, the search is sent to Google Search. These Google services follow Google's own privacy policy. Signature Digital does not receive this information.

Profiles are not incognito

Profiles keep website data separate from each other, but they are not private or incognito browsing. A profile keeps its cookies, site data, and page history until you clear or delete it.

Downloads

Regular website downloads are saved in Android's public Downloads folder. Generated website files can use the folder you select in Downloads settings. Android's folder picker gives access only to that folder. Downloaded files are outside profile storage and are not removed when you clear a profile or uninstall the app.

Backups and security

Back up all creates a password-protected AES-256-GCM backup, including saved page history. The app does not store the password; a forgotten password cannot be recovered. Active website data is protected by Android's app sandbox and device security, but the app does not add its own encryption to that live data. Keep your device locked.

HTTP pages do not encrypt network traffic. The browser asks before opening an HTTP page or downloading from one. If you continue, other people on the network may be able to read or change that traffic.

Deleting data

Clear all data removes cookies, cache, website storage, and history for the current profile only. It does not remove downloaded files. Deleting a profile removes only that profile's browser data. Uninstalling removes app-private data, but downloaded files and exported backups must be removed separately.

Purchases

Google Play processes subscription payments. The app receives purchase status to enable unlimited profile creation, not payment card details. Subscriptions renew until canceled in Google Play.

Children

This is a general-purpose web browser and is not directed to children under 13. Websites you visit may contain content that is not suitable for children.
