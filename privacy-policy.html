Privacy Policy

App: Solara Browser

Developer: Signature Digital

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Solara Browser is developed by Signature Digital. It allows you to browse websites using separate browsing profiles and Incognito mode on your Android device.

Solara Browser stores browsing information locally on your device. Signature Digital does not collect or store your personal browsing data on its servers.

1. What Is Stored

Each regular browsing profile stores its own cookies, cache, website storage, and page history on your device.

Browsing history is kept in the app's private storage. Website storage and cookies remain associated with their profile until you clear its data or delete the profile. Android may automatically remove temporary cache files.

Solara Browser does not send your browsing history to Signature Digital servers.

Websites you visit receive the requests, cookies, and information necessary to display their pages. Third-party cookies are enabled for compatibility, so websites and their partners may recognize activity across their services.

If you choose a file to upload, the website receiving the upload will have access to that file.

Website storage sizes shown by Solara Browser are estimates. Android WebView does not report the complete size of each website's cache or list every cookie domain.

2. Safe Browsing and Search

Android WebView may check the addresses of websites you visit using Google Safe Browsing to help warn you about potentially dangerous websites.

When you enter search terms instead of a website address, your search query is sent to Google Search.

These services are provided by Google and are subject to Google's Privacy Policy .

Signature Digital does not receive your search queries or Google Safe Browsing information.

3. Browsing Profiles

Regular browsing profiles keep website data separate from one another.

However, regular profiles are not Incognito sessions. Each profile retains its cookies, website storage, cache, and browsing history until you clear its data or delete it.

Profiles help organize and separate browsing sessions but do not make your browsing activity anonymous.

4. Incognito Mode

Solara Browser includes an Incognito mode for temporary browsing.

Incognito browsing data, including cookies, website storage, and browsing history, is deleted when the last Incognito tab is closed.

Incognito mode does not make you anonymous online. Websites, internet service providers, and network administrators may still be able to observe your activity.

Files downloaded during Incognito browsing may remain on your device and must be deleted separately.

5. Downloads

Regular website downloads are saved in Android's public Downloads folder.

Generated website files may be saved to a folder you select in the app's Downloads settings. Android's folder picker grants access only to the selected folder.

Downloaded files are stored outside browsing profile storage.

Clearing a profile, closing an Incognito session, or uninstalling Solara Browser does not automatically remove downloaded files.

Downloaded files must be deleted separately.

6. Backups and Security

Solara Browser allows you to create password-protected backups of your browsing profiles.

The Back Up All feature creates an encrypted backup using AES-256-GCM encryption.

Backups may contain:

  • Browsing profiles and saved page history.
  • Website cookies.
  • Website storage and locally stored website data.
  • Saved website sign-in sessions and authentication information contained in cookies or website storage.

Because backups may contain sensitive information, you should keep backup files and passwords secure.

Solara Browser does not store your backup password. If you forget it, the password cannot be recovered.

When restoring a backup, you can choose to replace existing profiles or add the backed-up profiles.

Some websites may require you to sign in again after restoration.

Active website data is protected by Android's application sandbox and device security features. Solara Browser does not apply additional application-level encryption to live browsing data.

We recommend keeping your device protected with a screen lock.

HTTP Connections

HTTP websites do not encrypt network traffic.

Solara Browser asks for confirmation before opening an HTTP website or downloading content over HTTP.

If you choose to continue, other parties on the network may be able to read or modify the transmitted information.

7. Deleting Data

Solara Browser provides options to delete browsing information stored on your device.

  • Clear All Data: Removes cookies, cache, website storage, and browsing history for the currently selected profile only.
  • Delete Profile: Removes the selected browsing profile and its associated website data.
  • Incognito Mode: Deletes Incognito browsing data automatically when the last Incognito tab is closed.
  • Uninstalling Solara Browser: Removes app-private browsing data from your device, subject to Android's backup and restore behavior.

Downloaded files and exported backup files are stored separately and must be removed manually.

Your privacy: Signature Digital does not collect or store your personal browsing data on its servers. Therefore, Signature Digital has no server-side personal browsing data to delete upon request.

8. Purchases and Premium Features

Solara Browser offers optional Premium features through Google Play subscriptions.

Google Play handles subscription payments, billing information, renewals, and cancellations.

Solara Browser receives purchase status information from Google Play to enable Premium features, including unlimited profile creation.

The app also stores your Premium entitlement status locally on your device to remember whether Premium features are enabled.

Signature Digital does not receive your payment card information.

Subscriptions automatically renew unless canceled through Google Play.

You can manage or cancel your subscription through Google Play Subscriptions .

9. App Updates

Solara Browser may check Google Play to determine whether a newer version of the app is available.

Google Play handles app update availability, downloads, and installation according to your device settings and Google Play preferences.

Signature Digital does not receive your browsing history through these update checks.

10. Children's Privacy

Solara Browser is a general-purpose web browser and is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13.

Websites accessed through the browser may contain content that is inappropriate for children.

Parents and guardians are encouraged to supervise children's internet activity.
